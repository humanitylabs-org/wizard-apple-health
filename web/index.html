Open Health

Your sleep, heart and activity show in the Health app. New data arrives when your iPhone is unlocked and Tailscale is on.

Your iPhone sends its Apple Health data to this Wizard with the app Health Auto Export. Four steps, all on your iPhone.

  1. Health Auto Export is an iPhone app by HealthyApps (not by Apple or Wizard). Sending data to your Wizard needs its Premium tier: $0.99 a month, $5.99 a year or $24.99 once, paid through the App Store.

    1. Install Health Auto Export on the iPhone that has your Apple Health data.
    2. Open it and allow it to read Health (tap Turn On All, then Allow).
    3. Buy Premium inside the app.
    Open in the App Store

  2. Your Wizard is private: only your own devices on Tailscale can reach it. Nothing is sent over the open internet.

    1. Install Tailscale on the iPhone (skip this if the Wizard app already works there).
    2. Sign in with the same account you use for this Wizard and switch Tailscale on.
    3. Check it: open this address in Safari on the iPhone. It should say "Your iPhone can reach your Wizard".
    Check address
    Get Tailscale

  3. In Health Auto Export open the menu, tap Automations, then New Automation, and choose REST API. Fill it in with these values; each has a Copy button.

      Workouts too (optional, a second automation)

      Health Auto Export sends one kind of data per automation. For workouts, create another REST API automation with the same URL and header, and these settings:

      1. In the automation, tap Manual Export.
      2. Choose a date range. Pick the last few months once to bring in your history.
      3. Tap Export and keep the iPhone unlocked until it finishes.
      Waiting for your iPhone…This updates by itself.

      Open Health

      After this, Health Auto Export sends new data by itself about every hour while your iPhone is unlocked.

    Connection details

    Rotate the token?

    The old token stops working at once. Your iPhone sends nothing until you paste the new header value into Health Auto Export (step 3). Your data here stays.

    Disconnect Apple Health?

    The app deletes everything your iPhone sent here and its token. Health stops showing Apple Health data. Nothing on your iPhone changes; delete the automation in Health Auto Export too.
